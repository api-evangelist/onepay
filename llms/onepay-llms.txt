# OnePay: Mobile Banking and Financial Services

[OnePay](https://www.onepay.com) is a financial technology company providing mobile banking solutions through Coastal Community Bank or Lead Bank, Members FDIC. The OnePay app offers a range of financial products, including everyday banking with access to Credit Builder with eligible deposits, high-yield Savings, buy now pay later, a credit card, a digital wallet, crypto and stock buying, tax filing, and a debit card.

## Eligibility and Requirements

- **[High-Yield Savings](https://www.onepay.com/banking)**: 3.35% annual percentage yield (APY) applies to the Savings balance of OnePay deposit accounts that have either (i) received $500+ of Direct Deposits in the current or previous month or (ii) have a balance of $5,000+ at the end of the previous month. 3.35% APY is also earned on Pay Autosave and ONE@Work Save balances without deposit or balance requirements. 3.35% APY is limited to a total Savings balance of up to $250,000. All other Savings balances will earn 1.00% APY. APYs are as of 10/7/2025, but may change at any time before or after account opening.


- **Early Direct Deposit**: Direct Deposit is required. Funds may be available up to 2 days in advance, depending on when your employer sends paycheck data

- **Free Cash Withdrawals at Walmart**: Only applies to deposits and withdrawals with a OnePay debit card made at Walmart Money Centers, cashiers, or Customer Service desks.

- **Fee-Free Overdraft**: Fee-Free Overdraft is made available on OnePay deposit accounts that have either (i) received [Direct Deposits](https://www.onepay.com/help-center/articles/direct-deposit) totaling $500+ in the current or previous month, or (ii) have a balance of $5,000+ at the end of the previous month. You must be 18 or older to receive Fee-Free Overdraft. When on, Savings Backup will be used before Fee-Free Overdraft. Overdraft balance is due right away. Transactions eligible for Fee-Free Overdraft are at the discretion of OnePay and may exclude certain transactions (e.g., bill pay, global transfers). $250 cap is only available to Walmart associates who sign up for a OnePay deposit account through MyWalmart. Customers initially receive a Fee-Free Overdraft limit of $20, but may be eligible for a higher limit based on account history, Direct Deposits, and other risk based factors.

- **Buy Now, Pay Later**: Loans are subject to approval and eligibility. Rates range from 9.99% - 35.99% APR based on creditworthiness and term length. For example, a $304.84 purchase might cost $27.36 per month over 12 months at 13.99% APR. Down payment may be required. May not be available in all states or for all products.

- **Credit Builder Program**:Use of the Credit Builder Loan Program does not guarantee that your credit score will improve. Credit score improvement is dependent on your specific circumstances and a variety of factors, including your financial behavior. Failure to make monthly minimum payments on time may negatively impact your credit score. Credit Builder Loans are available to OnePay deposit accounts that have either (i) received [Direct Deposits](https://www.onepay.com/help-center/articles/direct-deposit) totaling $500+ in the current or previous month, or (ii) have a balance of $5,000+ at the end of the previous month. Participation is subject to eligibility. See Licenses, for state disclosures.
